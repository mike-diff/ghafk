# Security

ghafk runs coding agents with your credentials, and issue text is their
prompt. The [security model](docs/security.md) tells you what ghafk
prevents and what it leaves to you.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately through this repository's **Security** tab, using
**Report a vulnerability**. Please do not open a public issue.

Useful reports show how text or state from someone without write access
can reach an agent, a shell, a merge or your credentials, or how ghafk can
be made to spend tokens without limit.
